Additional supporting evidence can be submitted either online on by post.

Uploading additional supporting evidence online

  • Go to GOV.UK/PIP
  • Log in using the details from when they applied.
  • Select 'Upload evidence to support your PIP claim'.

Advise the person:

  • to write their full name and reference number on the top of each photocopy they send

Explain what will happen next

  • They will get a letter to let them know that their mandatory reconsideration has been registered.
  • We will also send them a copy of their decision letter.
  • As they did not want to send any additional supporting evidence, their decision will be reconsidered based on what we currently know and understand.
  • Someone will look at all the information again and make another decision.
  • They will get a letter called a Mandatory Reconsideration Notice telling them whether we've changed the decision.
  • They should receive this within 15 weeks.
