Explain how to download the form online

Advise the person:

  • to go to GOV.UK and use the magnifying glass in the top right corner to search for CRMR1
  • that they can download the form and either print it or complete it on screen
  • that we will register that they've asked us to reconsider their decision
  • We will also send them a copy of their decision letter..

They should return the completed form either:

  • by post to the address at the top of their decision letter
  • by logging into their PIP account and and selecting 'Upload supporting evidence'

An entry will be added to the event history and decision overview.
